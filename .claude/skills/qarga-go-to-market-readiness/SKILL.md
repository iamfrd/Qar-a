# Qarğa Go-to-Market Readiness

This skill is for strategy and preparation only. Publishing content, connecting advertising accounts, launching campaigns, and spending money require separate human approval.

## Readiness gates

- the student search → detail → trial/registration flow is stable on the real backend;
- provider supply and course-data quality are sufficient;
- the value proposition and primary persona are clear;
- analytics events and conversion measurement exist;
- a support and complaint-escalation model exists;
- revenue/commission and refund principles are clear;
- minimum privacy/terms drafts and operational owners exist.

## Strategy deliverable

- persona and problem;
- positioning and differentiation hypothesis;
- supply-side and demand-side launch sequence;
- channel hypotheses: SEO/content, provider partnerships, referrals, paid media;
- prerequisites, test hypothesis, and measurement for every channel;
- product backlog required before campaigns start;
- a “do not do yet” list.

Prepare the product and measurement system first. Base channel selection on the target audience and evidence, not trends or personal preference.
