# Qarğa Marketplace Monetization

Starting business hypothesis: Qarğa earns revenue by bringing students and registrations to course providers. The exact model is not approved; Qarğa receiving payments itself and charging providers later through commission or invoices are legally and operationally different models.

## Ask before every monetization decision

- Who pays: the student, the provider, or both?
- When is value created: lead, trial reservation, confirmed registration, payment, or attendance?
- Does Qarğa hold or transfer money, or only calculate commission?
- Who bears cancellation/refund and no-show risk?
- What measurable value does the provider receive?
- Is there evidence from competitors, alternatives, or willingness to pay?

## Models to investigate

- percentage commission from successful registration;
- fixed fee for every confirmed lead/registration;
- provider subscription/plan;
- sponsored visibility with clear labels and ranking guardrails;
- hybrid model: low platform fee + transaction fee;
- manual reconciliation during a pilot.

Never invent a percentage or price. Compare options across unit economics, provider incentives, student-price impact, refund load, fraud, and reconciliation.

## Recommended sequence

1. Select the value event.
2. Build event and audit data.
3. Plan a manual pilot with a small provider group.
4. Give commission/refund contract drafts to the legal agent.
5. Build payment architecture only after the business model is approved.
6. Measure conversion, provider retention, disputes, and margin.

## Pricing research discipline

Before proposing provider pricing or commission mechanics, separate value metric, packaging, willingness-to-pay evidence, competitor/alternative evidence, provider incentives, payment fees, support/refund cost, and margin assumptions. Use ranges or scenarios only when inputs are explicitly labeled assumptions. Prefer a bounded manual pilot over a permanent rate when evidence is weak. Never copy SaaS pricing patterns without adapting them to a two-sided course marketplace.
