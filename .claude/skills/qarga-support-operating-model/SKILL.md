# Qarğa Support Operating Model

Support serves two sides: students and course providers. Admin screens, statuses, and audit trails are product components of support—not merely message handling.

## Minimum model

- channels and operating hours;
- ticket categories: account/OTP, course information, trial, registration, payment/refund, provider dispute, security, and data/privacy;
- severity and escalation;
- responsible owner: support, provider, finance/payment, security, or legal;
- response and resolution targets—assign numbers only after business approval;
- knowledge base and prepared responses;
- PII redaction and audit logs;
- status visibility for providers and students;
- product-feedback loop for repeated problems.

## Dedicated-agent trigger

The coordinator may propose a `qarga-customer-support-strategist` role when the following repeat:

- the support workflow becomes a separate milestone;
- the same ticket category exceeds product/engineering scope in at least two tasks;
- payment/refund and provider escalation require a permanent owner;
- the knowledge base and support dashboard require ongoing management.
