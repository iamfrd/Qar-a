---
description: Independently compare a skill or prompt candidate against its baseline on validation and hidden-holdout capability cases, then record regression-aware evidence.
---

Use `qarga-evaluation-engineer`. Verify diagnostic, validation, and holdout sets are disjoint; keep holdout answers away from the candidate author; evaluate baseline and candidate under comparable conditions; score objective requirements; check critical failures and category regressions; record cost/runtime evidence when available; and output ADVANCE_TO_PILOT, REVISE, NO_PROVEN_GAIN, or REJECT. Do not promote the candidate directly.
