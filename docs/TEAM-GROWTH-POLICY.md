# Team Growth Policy

## Review triggers

Run a capability/team review after a major milestone, when the same gap appears in at least two independent tasks, when a role repeatedly works outside its boundary, before payment/launch/scaled-support stages, or when learning/evolution evidence identifies a persistent ownerless responsibility.

## Decision order

Before proposing a new agent, test the smallest sufficient alternative:

1. clearer requirement or documentation;
2. checklist or completion-contract improvement;
3. better routing/handoff/context;
4. existing skill improvement;
5. new skill or command;
6. tool/integration/hook with minimal permissions;
7. only then a new long-lived specialist agent.

## Hiring proposal

A proposal must state the repeated evidence, responsibility boundary, why existing roles cannot own it, alternatives considered, expected benefit, coordination/context cost, minimum tools/permissions, reporting/reviewer model, success criteria, pilot plan, and retirement criteria.

The coordinator may create a new agent only after explicit project-owner approval. Implementation must go through the controlled system-evolution path, update the registry and coordinator allowlist, initialize governance/scorecard data, pass validation/security review, and then begin in a new Claude session.

Current future-role candidates are defined only in `.claude/capability-registry.json`; this document does not duplicate their triggers.
